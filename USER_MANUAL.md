# Referitup - Platform User Manual & Operating Guide

Welcome to **Referitup**, the enterprise affiliate referral management platform. This manual provides a complete walkthrough for **Affiliate Publishers** and **Platform Administrators**.

---

## 1. System Overview & Architecture

Referitup connects publishers with high-paying product campaigns. Publishers earn instant flat-rate or percentage commissions by sharing unique referral links.

### Key Workflows:
- **Referred User Flow**: User 1 shares a referral link -> User 2 clicks link -> Enters Login page -> Authenticates -> Enters Profile details (Name & 10-digit mobile number) -> **Redirected directly to the target Product Link**.
- **Direct Visitor Flow**: User visits website directly -> Authenticates -> Enters Profile details -> **Redirected to User Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Affiliate Publisher Guide

### 2.1 Registration & Onboarding
1. Click **Sign Up** or open a referral invitation link (`/join?ref=YOUR_CODE`).
2. Log in with your **Email** or **Google 1-Tap OAuth**.
3. Complete your profile details:
   - **Full Name**: Your legal name for payout verification.
   - **Mobile Phone Number**: 10-digit Indian mobile number (starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9).
4. Upon clicking **Save Details & Enter Portal**, your account is instantly activated.

### 2.2 Sharing Referral Links & Earning Commissions
1. Navigate to **Refer & Earn** or **Marketplace** from the sidebar.
2. Locate your **Exclusive Affiliate Code** (e.g., `REF-KISHORE-2026`).
3. Click **Copy URL** or select a product:
   - **Default Shareable URL**: `https://your-domain.com/join?ref=YOUR_CODE`
   - **Product-Specific URL**: `https://your-domain.com/p/prod-1?ref=YOUR_CODE`
4. Use the **WhatsApp** or **Share Link** buttons for 1-click sharing.
5. Earn **₹10.00** instant referral commission for every verified signup or sale driven through your link.
6. **Sprint Target Bonus**: After completing **7 referrals**, earn an additional **₹30.00 cash bonus** credited straight to your earnings balance!

### 2.3 Wallet, Earnings & Payouts
1. Go to **Wallet** or **Withdrawals** in the navigation menu.
2. View your **Available Balance**, **Pending Earnings**, and **Total Earnings**.
3. Request Payout:
   - Enter payout amount (minimum threshold applies).
   - Provide your **UPI ID** (e.g., `user@upi`) or **Bank Account Details**.
   - Payout requests are processed directly to your bank account.

## 3. Administrator Guide

### 3.1 Accessing the Admin Portal
1. Navigate to `/admin/login`.
2. Log in with your Administrator credentials.
3. Access real-time analytics on GMV, Active Users, Payout Requests, and Fraud Monitoring.

### 3.2 Managing Products & Marketplace
1. Go to **Admin -> Products**.
2. Add new products, update prices, commission rates, and external product destination links.
3. Toggle product status between **Active** and **Inactive**.

### 3.3 Referral & Conversion Management
1. Go to **Admin -> Referrals** and **Admin -> Conversions**.
2. Audit referred clients, traffic clicks, attribution timestamps, and commission statuses.
3. Approve or reject pending referral payouts.

### 3.4 Withdrawal Approvals
1. Navigate to **Admin -> Withdrawals**.
2. Review pending withdrawal requests from publishers.
3. Click **Approve** or **Batch Approve All** to disburse funds.

### 3.5 Fraud Risk & Security Controls
1. Access **Admin -> Fraud Risk**.
2. Inspect IP duplication alerts, self-referral attempts, and multi-account detections.
3. Review audit logs for system security and compliance.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Why didn't a referral show up in my stats?
- Ensure the referred user registered/logged in using your exact referral link (`?ref=YOUR_CODE`). Self-referrals (referring your own email) are automatically flagged by security filters.

### Q: How long are referral cookies valid?
- Referitup uses a 90-day tracking cookie window.

### Q: What is the OTP verification rule?
- OTP verification has been streamlined into direct 10-digit mobile number validation for instant registration without waiting for SMS codes.
